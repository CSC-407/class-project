ABOUT PROJECTVAULT

Making Academic Research Easier to Discover

ProjectVault is a web-based academic project repository designed to provide a central platform for exploring and discovering final-year student projects.

The platform aims to make academic research more accessible by organising projects according to their departments, topics and academic years.
